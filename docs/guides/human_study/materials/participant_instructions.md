# Participant Instructions (read aloud / show on screen)

Thank you for taking part. This takes about 35 minutes. There are no trick questions about you; we are evaluating the tool, not you.

## Your task
You are advising a finance ministry. For each of 6 cases (a country and year) you will:

1. **See the context:** last year's budget split between four categories: Health, Education, Defence, Other.
2. **Propose your own split** for the coming year (four percentages that add up to 100%). Rate your confidence (1-5).
3. **See the AI system's output.** Depending on your group this may be just the recommended split, or also explanations. Read as much as you find useful.
4. **Give your final split** (you may keep or change your first answer) and rate your confidence again.

Your goal is to match, as closely as possible, the allocation the government actually adopted. The AI system can be wrong; use your own judgement.

## Rules
- Work alone; do not use the internet or other tools.
- Take the time you need, but aim for about 3 minutes per case.
- You may ask the facilitator about how the interface works, but not for the "right answer".
- You can stop at any time.

## Practice
You will first do one practice case that is not scored.

## Afterwards
You will fill in short questionnaires (trust, usability, workload, a 5-question quiz about the explanations).
